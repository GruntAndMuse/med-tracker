# Quickstart — Med Tracker

Get from zero to tracking your first pill in about 2 minutes. No account, no install, no internet needed after the first open.

## 1. Open it

Open `index.html` on your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). That's the whole app — one file.

**Make it feel like an app:** tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Now it opens full-screen like a real app.

## 2. Add your first pill

1. Tap **My Pills** at the bottom, then the big **+** button.
2. Tap **📷 Photo of Pill** — take a picture of one pill. This is your visual reference so you can match the real pill to the photo later.
3. Tap **🏷️ Photo of Label** — take a picture of the bottle label. Pills can look alike; the label tells them apart.
4. Type the **pill name** (e.g. Zofran) and **dose** (e.g. 8mg).
5. Tap the **times** you take it. ☀️ morning times are yellow, 🌙 evening times are blue.
6. Tap **With food**, **Empty stomach**, or **Either**.
7. Set **how many pills you have** (or tap "Don't track" to skip counting).
8. Tap **✅ Save Pill**.

## 3. Check off today's doses

The **Today** tab shows every dose due today, grouped under **☀️ Morning** and **🌙 Evening** headers. Tap the big circle when you take one — it turns green with a ✓.

- Tap the **pill photo** on any dose to see it full-screen and compare it to the pill in your hand.
- Feeling nauseous? Tap **⏰ 30 min** or **⏰ 1 hour** to snooze a dose instead of skipping it.
- If you accidentally tap a dose twice, the app warns you before letting you double-dose.

## 4. Add a doctor visit

Tap **Doctors** → **+**. Enter the date, time, doctor name, and any notes (e.g. "bring lab results"). Upcoming visits show at the top.

## 5. Back up (do this once)

Tap **More** → **📥 Save Backup**. Keep the file somewhere safe (email it to yourself). If you ever lose your phone data, **📤 Load Backup** restores everything.

---

**That's it.** The app works offline, keeps everything on your phone, and never asks you to log in or pay for anything.
