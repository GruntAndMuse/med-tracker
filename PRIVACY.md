# Privacy Policy — Med Tracker

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

## The short version

We collect nothing. There is nowhere to send it.

## The long version

Med Tracker stores everything you enter — medication names, photos, dose times, appointment details, history — in your phone's own browser storage (`localStorage`). It never transmits any of it anywhere, because the app contains no network code, no analytics, no advertising libraries, no accounts, and no servers.

Specifically:

- **No data collection.** We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your information. It physically cannot reach us.
- **No accounts or login.** There is no sign-up, no password, nothing identifying you to us.
- **No advertising or tracking.** No ad SDKs, no analytics, no cookies for tracking, no device fingerprinting.
- **No third parties.** No data is shared with anyone, because no data leaves your device.
- **Photos stay on your phone.** Pill and label photos are stored as compressed images in your phone's browser storage only.
- **Children.** The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Your data, your control

- **Backup:** More → Save Backup exports everything to a file you keep.
- **Delete:** uninstalling the app or clearing the site's browser data removes everything. There is no server copy to request deletion of, because none exists.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional feature involving data transmission), the policy will be updated here before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the changelog.
