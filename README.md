# Med Tracker

Free pill tracker that's private by design. No login, no ads, nothing leaves your phone.

Built for chemo patients and anyone who needs simple medication tracking.

## Features

- 📷 **Photo of each pill + bottle label** — tell look-alike pills apart visually
- ☀️🌙 **Morning/evening color coding** — never mix up AM and PM
- 🍽️ **With-food / empty-stomach badges** — right on the dose card
- ✅ **Big tap-to-check daily checklist**
- ⏰ **Snooze** — remind me in 30 min or 1 hour
- ⚠️ **Double-dose guard** — warns if you try to take something you already took
- 💊 **Pill count with refill warnings**
- 📋 **Doctor appointment tracker**
- 📥 **JSON backup** — export/import everything
- 🔒 **Private by design** — all data stays on your phone, works offline

## Privacy

No login. No account. No ads. No tracking. No cloud. No data leaves your device. Free forever.

This is a reminder helper only — not medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.

## Use it

Open `index.html` on any phone browser (iPhone or Android). Add to home screen for an app-like experience. Works offline after first load.
